# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Educators with a few minutes on a mobile device who want to notice and reduce avoidable barriers in their own learning environments.

## Product Purpose

Designed for Difference is a reflective, privacy-first learning experience. It moves educators from recognising their own everyday variation to making one practical environmental change.

## Brand Personality

Thoughtful, direct, hopeful. It should feel human and spacious, never clinical, institutional, or compliance-driven.

## Anti-references

Medical screeners, diagnostic quizzes, dense LMS dashboards, and accessibility compliance checklists.

## Design Principles

- Recognition before instruction
- Environment before deficit
- One clear action at a time
- Agency through small, practical experiments
- Model the accessibility being advocated

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use semantic structure, keyboard support, visible focus, generous touch targets, responsive type, and reduced-motion alternatives.
