# Design System

## Intent

Quiet confidence in a bright staffroom between sessions: clear daylight, a considered note in the margin, and one useful idea to try tomorrow.

## Color strategy

Restrained product palette. Teal provides direction and deep plum provides the moment of reflection; neutral white keeps the experience legible.

## Tokens

- Background: `oklch(1 0 0)`
- Surface: `oklch(0.975 0.008 188)`
- Ink: `oklch(0.22 0.025 205)`
- Muted: `oklch(0.43 0.026 205)`
- Primary: `oklch(0.47 0.12 188)`
- Accent: `oklch(0.36 0.11 326)`

## Typography

System sans-serif stack, with a compact product scale and large, balanced question headings.
